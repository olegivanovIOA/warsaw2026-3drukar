Technology

Series FDM printing vs. conventional injection moulding

Series FDM printing can be a smarter choice.

CriterionInjection mouldingSeries FDM printing
Tooling investment€3,000–€20,000+€0
Production launch time3–8 weeks2–4 days
Design changesAdditional tooling costs€0, digital changes
Minimum order quantityOften 1,000+ unitsFlexible quantities, from 100 units
Prototype productionSeparate tooling and processFree test prints
Production scalabilityAdditional moulds may be required5,300+ printers available

For very high volumes of simple parts, injection moulding can offer a lower cost per unit. Our advantage is a fast start, no tooling costs and the ability to modify designs quickly.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, before you share drawings if you prefer. Every custom formulation is covered by an NDA as standard.

Can you produce in Poland?

Yes. 500+ industrial printers already run in Poland, so EU orders and samples ship without cross-border delays.

Which materials do you print?

PETG, PLA and ABS from our own DRUKAR line. When you need specific mechanical, thermal, UV or flame-retardant properties, PLEXIWIRE develops a compound for your part.

What is the minimum order?

Flexible quantities from 100 units. There is no mould to pay off, and our farms handle series from 10,000 up to 1,000,000 parts.

How fast can you start?

First a free test print or filament sample, then a same-day quote. Production launches in 2–4 days.

Can you produce under our brand?

Yes. We print parts and make filament for OEM and private-label customers, including packaging and labelling.
